How verification works here

You can verify addresses two ways: locally with the built-in SMTP pipeline (below), or through an external service (ZeroBounce, NeverBounce, etc.) by importing results further down. A deliverable result becomes delivery-confirmed evidence and sharpens the firm's pattern; an undeliverable one is dropped from inference; catch_all is recorded but proves nothing.

  1. Export candidates to verify (CSV) — generated addresses not yet checked.
  2. Run that CSV through your verification service and download its results.
  3. Import the results below, then re-run leadcore infer (or it re-runs on next infer).
